I am Jiao Yuan, a Master's student in Bioinformatics at [Sichuan University](https://www.scu.edu.cn/). I use computational and machine-learning methods to investigate fundamental questions in life sciences, and I received my B.Eng. in Environmental Science and Engineering from [Chengdu University of Technology](https://www.cdut.edu.cn/) in 2025.

Currently, I focus on (1) scRNA-seq to dissect developmental regulation and meiotic crossover landscape in *Populus alba* var. *pyramidalis*, (2) explainable deep learning for phenotyping and trait prediction in *Idesia polycarpa*, and (3) development of bioinformatics tools for long-read transcriptome and methylome analysis.

I write about bioinformatics, tooling and life as an Arch Linux user. My code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/imjiaoyuan), I post occasional essays on WeChat (猫四叔), or reach me at imjiaoyuan [at] gmail.com.
